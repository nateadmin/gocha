Gocha is your mobile-first super app for messaging, AI-powered catch-up, and local discovery.

Chat with people and businesses in one inbox. Pin important threads, filter unread chats, and stay organized with lists and labels.

Gocha AI is your built-in assistant for planning, writing, and everyday questions.

Catch Up uses AI to summarize what matters across your conversations so you can reply faster.

AI features are off until you turn them on. When on, the text of your messages is sent to our AI provider, OpenAI, for translation, summaries, and Gocha AI replies. You can turn them off any time in Settings.

Discover nearby groups and local businesses. Explore menus, offers, and community posts.

Share 24-hour text statuses.

Create profile cards you control for professional and personal use.

Sign in with email, switch accounts, and choose your language.

Stay safe: report messages or people, block anyone, and delete your account any time from Settings.

Gocha is also available on the web at https://app.gocha.ai.
