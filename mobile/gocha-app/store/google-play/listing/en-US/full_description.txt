CHAT WITH PEOPLE AND BUSINESSES
Message friends, groups, and businesses in one inbox. Pin important threads, filter unread chats, and stay organized with lists and labels.

GOCHA AI
Your built-in assistant helps you plan, write, and get things done without leaving the app.

CATCH UP
AI briefings summarize what matters across your conversations so you can reply faster.

DISCOVER
Find nearby groups and local businesses. Explore menus, offers, and community posts.

STATUSES
Share text, photo, and video updates that disappear after 24 hours.

PROFILE CARDS
Create professional, match, and custom profile cards you control.

ACCOUNT AND PRIVACY
Sign in with email or phone. Switch accounts, manage your language, and control how you appear to others.
